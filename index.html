360° SaaS Platform Documentation

A comprehensive, all-in-one cloud platform designed to unify customer data, automate business processes, and deliver actionable insights across your entire organization.

1. Introduction

The 360° SaaS Platform provides a unified ecosystem for managing customer relationships, sales pipelines, marketing automation, analytics, and integrations. Built with scalability and security in mind, it serves teams from startups to enterprise.

1.1 Key Benefits

  • Unified Data Model: Single source of truth for all customer interactions and business objects.
  • No-Code Automation: Build complex workflows with visual builders without engineering resources.
  • Enterprise Security: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant infrastructure.
  • Extensible API: RESTful and GraphQL APIs with 200+ pre-built connectors.

2. Platform Architecture

Our multi-tenant architecture ensures data isolation while maintaining performance at scale. All services are containerized and deployed on Kubernetes.

3. Getting Started

Follow these steps to provision your workspace and invite your team.

3.1 Account Setup

  1. Sign up at app.360saas.io/signup with your work email.
  2. Verify your domain to enable SSO and email automations.
  3. Choose your data region: US East, EU West, or AP Southeast.
  4. Complete the onboarding checklist to import existing data.
Note: All new workspaces start with a 14-day trial of our Professional plan. No credit card required.

3.2 API Authentication

All API requests must include a bearer token. Generate tokens from Settings → Developers → API Keys.

curl -X GET https://api.360saas.io/v1/contacts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json"

4. Data Model & Objects

The platform ships with standard objects and supports unlimited custom objects. All objects support custom fields, validation rules, and layouts.

4.1 Standard Objects

  • Contact: Individuals with email, phone, and social profiles.
  • Company: Organizations with hierarchy, revenue, and firmographics.
  • Deal: Opportunities with stage, amount, close date, and owner.
  • Ticket: Support cases with priority, status, and SLA tracking.
  • Activity: Tasks, calls, meetings, and notes across all objects.
